Privacy Policy — Rain on Ivory Uploader

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Rain on Ivory Uploader ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Rain on Ivory (@RainOnIvoryPiano). It is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own original videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

1. What data the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App receives an access token for the owner's own YouTube channel with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the channel's own videos
  • youtube — to set video details, thumbnails, schedules and playlists on the channel

The App does not access any other Google data, and it does not access data of any other person.

2. How the data is used

The access is used only to upload and manage videos on the Rain on Ivory channel. The App does not read, collect or analyse viewers' personal data.

3. Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to any third party, and it is not stored on any server.

4. Sharing

No user data is shared with anyone. The App uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the owner's computer.
